The Vision

Switzerland has long been associated with stability, quality, and trust in global finance. Despite this, recent developments have shown that even strong systems are not immune to setbacks, and that reputations must be continuously defended.

The Quantitative Student Fund Zurich is part of a new generation of talented minds, recognizing a growing need for renewed invention and for individuals willing to take on the responsibility of upholding Switzerland’s global reputation. It is our mission to contribute to the image of Swiss quality, with accountability towards investors, customers, and business partners.

Located in Zurich, a special city that harbours immense talent in technology as well as in finance,there is immense high-potential for invention and creativity. Out of this environment formed the Quantitative Student Fund Zurich, a collective of innovative, cutting-edge projects in the field of quantitative finance. In the fast-paced modern world, operating dynamically in small teams is a crucial ingredient for success.

It is our vision to drive a new wave of economic and technological progress in the field of quantitative finance to counter stagnation and reinforce Switzerland’s reputation. It is our Vision to establishing a new pillar among Switzerlands defining industries.
